OUR STORY

BORN IN DETROIT. BUILT THROUGH RESILIENCE. CREATED FOR COMMUNITY.

Every great brand starts with a story.

TastyStrains started in Detroit.

Born and raised on Fenkell and Monte Vista, the founder grew up in a community that taught the importance of family, hard work, perseverance, and looking out for one another.

Growing up, the founder's parents were strong providers who worked hard to give their family a good life. When her father was sentenced to 20 years in prison, it deeply impacted the family and became one of the defining challenges of her childhood.

Yet, even during difficult times, there were moments of happiness that left a lasting impression.

One of those cherished memories was helping an uncle pass out ice cream from his truck. The smiles on people's faces, the excitement throughout the neighborhood, and the simple joy that a frozen treat could bring to the community became a memory that never faded.

At the time, it was simply a childhood experience. Years later, it became part of the inspiration behind TastyStrains.

FROM A CHILDHOOD MEMORY TO A BUSINESS

In 2021, TastyStrains was founded with a vision to transform that feeling of community and joy into a brand.

TastyStrains is a pre-packaged frozen dessert brand inspired by community, creativity, bold flavors, and resilience.

What began with a childhood memory became a mission to bring excitement and joy back to neighborhoods like the one that raised its founder.

Every cup represents more than a frozen dessert. It represents resilience, creativity, and the belief that difficult circumstances do not have to determine the future.

TastyStrains is proof that even the smallest moments can inspire something much bigger.

ROOTED IN DETROIT. BUILT FOR THE FUTURE.

Detroit will always be at the heart of TastyStrains.

The vision is to grow through local retail partnerships, reach more neighborhoods, and build TastyStrains into a recognizable brand throughout Detroit and beyond.

At its core, TastyStrains is about recreating that feeling from the neighborhood ice-cream truck—the excitement of receiving something cold, flavorful, and made to bring a smile to someone's face.

Because sometimes, a simple frozen treat becomes more than a treat.

It becomes a memory.

And sometimes, a memory becomes a mission.
